## Style should feel personal

Getting dressed is a small decision that shapes your whole day. But most style tools begin with shopping, trends, or someone else’s idea of what looks good.

Oro begins with you: your wardrobe, your taste, your plans, and the way you want to feel.

## A stylist in your corner

We’re building Oro to make thoughtful style advice easy to reach. You can ask what to wear, work through an outfit, or find a new way to use something you already own.

The goal is simple: less time second-guessing your clothes, and more time feeling like yourself in them.

## Built around your wardrobe

We believe personal style comes from understanding what works for you, not from buying more for the sake of it. That’s why Oro is designed around the clothes you already have and the life you actually live.

We’re still learning, listening, and improving. If you want to say hello, share feedback, or work with us, [get in touch](/contact).
