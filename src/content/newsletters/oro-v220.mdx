## Android users: you can finally download Oro.

**We're on the Google Play Store.** If you've been waiting since the beginning, the wait is over. Go download it.

[Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.buildingoro.app)

_Note: Oro+ is currently available for iOS only. Android support is coming in our next update._

---

## Weather-aware fits

Fit generation now pulls your local weather. **If it's 12°C and overcast, Oro isn't suggesting linen.** The recommendation adjusts for what you'd actually need to wear outside today.

_To get this, go to your profile and enable location access._ It's worth it.

---

## Your colours. Your body.

Every person dresses differently: not just in taste, but in how colour lands on them, how proportions work on their frame. **A fit that looks right on one person can look off on another wearing the same pieces.**

Oro now does a colour and body analysis. Your skin tone, your undertones, the proportions that work for you. **It factors into every fit Oro generates from here.**

> A recommendation is only useful if it's built for you, specifically.

If you've already set up your profile, you'll find it in settings. Takes a minute.

## More room, and a better try-on

If you've been hitting your monthly limit, **Oro+ is now available for iOS.** More fit generations, more uploads, more try-ons. The free plan isn't going anywhere; this is just more headroom if you want it.

And if your virtual try-on photo is outdated, **you can now swap it out from your profile.** The try-on is only as good as the photo you give it.
