For months, the bottom of every one of these letters has ended on the same quiet line: _Android coming soon._ A quiet little promise we kept asking you to take on faith.

**Not anymore.** Oro is on the Google Play Store, today, for real. If you've been waiting on the sidelines watching everyone else dress out of their own closets, you can stop waiting.

[Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.buildingoro.app)

_Available now on Android · free to start_

---

To the Android half of this list: thank you for being patient with us. Plenty of you replied to these emails, found us in the Discord, or just told us flatly that you'd download it the day it landed. _We remembered every one of those._ This one's for you.

![Gatsby reaching across the water for the green light](/newsletter-images/gatsby.gif)

> The fashionably late one has arrived.

Welcome in. Let's get you dressed.
