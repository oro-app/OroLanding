2.1.0 just went live. Two headlines: **Oro got a lot more personal,** and **you finally have the storage you've been asking for.** Here's what's new.

---

## Weather-aware fits

Fit generation now pulls your local weather. **If it's 12°C and overcast, Oro isn't suggesting linen.** The recommendation adjusts for what you'd actually need to wear outside today.

_To turn it on, enable location access in your profile._ It's worth it.

---

## Your colours. Your body.

Every person dresses differently: not just in taste, but in how colour lands on them, how proportions sit on their frame. **A fit that looks right on one person can look off on another in the same pieces.**

Oro now runs a colour and body analysis. Your skin tone, your undertones, the proportions that work for you. **It factors into every fit Oro generates from here.**

![The twelve seasonal colour families, arranged by warm, cool, light, dark, bright and soft](/newsletter-images/color-seasons.png)

> A recommendation is only useful if it's built for you, specifically.

Already set up your profile? You'll find it in settings. Takes a minute.

---

## More room. Finally.

The one you've been asking for: **more storage for your closet, with Oro+.** Upload far more of what you own, so Oro works from your whole wardrobe instead of a slice of it. **The fuller your closet in the app, the better every fit it builds.**

Oro+ lifts the rest of the caps too: the most fit generations, the most try-ons, headroom for the mornings you're dressing in a hurry. The free plan isn't going anywhere. This is for the people who live in the app.

---

## And a few other things

Bug fixes, performance improvements, more polish across the app, and you can now **swap your virtual try-on photo** from your profile. The try-on is only as good as the photo you give it.
