These production links become available when the poster openings are deployed.

| Dating | https://www.askoro.now/beta?src=poster-dating | Dating |

Append a lowercase placement suffix, such as `poster-career-waterloo-library`, to distinguish locations. Career and dating tags redirect to their matching `/beta/career` or `/beta/dating` opening, keeping query parameters; other poster tags use the general opening. Use `/beta` in QR links so scans enter the signup experience directly.

The current URL selects the opening. Signup attribution retains the existing first-source behavior, including a saved draft’s source. Referral codes remain separate and can be appended as `&ref=…`. Preview domains can use the same paths and tags.
